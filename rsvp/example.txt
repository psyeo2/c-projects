Rapid Serial Visual Presentation (RSVP) is a speed reading technique where
words are displayed one at a time at a fixed point on the screen, usually
aligned around an optimal recognition point within each word. By keeping the
text stationary, RSVP reduces eye movement and allows readers to process words
more quickly than with traditional left to right scanning. While this method
can significantly increase reading speed for simple narrative text, it may
reduce comprehension for complex material that benefits from rereading,
context, and natural pacing.
